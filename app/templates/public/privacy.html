Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated {{ now_year }}

This policy explains what {{ site_name }} collects, why, and what choices you have.

What we collect

We do not ask visitors to create accounts and we do not collect names or email addresses from readers. Our own server keeps standard web logs (IP address, browser user agent, requested page, timestamp) for security and troubleshooting.

If you write for this site, we store the account details you provide: username, email address, display name and biography.

Cookies

We set a small number of cookies:

  • Session cookie — only for signed-in writers, to keep them signed in. Readers never receive it.
  • Consent cookie — records your advertising choice so we do not ask on every page.

Advertising

We show advertising to fund the site.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and other sites.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

Where consent is required, advertising and measurement scripts are not loaded until you accept. If you decline, the site continues to work and no advertising cookies are set by us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Contact us and we will respond.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change, and revise the date above.
